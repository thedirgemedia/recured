recured

live visual instrument
Plays video files, images or a camera through a chain of real-time shaders, or runs the shaders on their own. Any parameter can be moved by audio, LFOs, a tempo clock or MIDI. Records the output as video and can show it on a second screen.
Panel
◀ tabOpens or closes the control panel.
grip stripTap to close the panel; drag to resize it.
SHADERS AND FX CHAINSFolds the preset bank and the GEN and FX rows, leaving PARAMS. Remembered.
PARAMS headerNames the selection and folds the sliders. Shows the clip's controls with the CLIP row selected, the camera's with CAM selected.
section headersEach heading folds its section. VIDEO, LFO, AUDIO INPUT, MIDI and MISC start closed. Remembered.
modesClips reach the picture as GEN blocks: clip layers (CL1–CL8) or a sequence (SEQ). The camera is a GEN block (CAM). With none, the shaders run alone. The panel title shows CLIPS, CAMERA, CLIPS + CAMERA or SHADERS.
RNDRandomises the GEN and FX rows (1–8 blocks each), their params, blend modes and amounts, the LFOs and some LFO bindings. Keeps CAM in box 1; with clips loaded, fills the STEPS row and adds a sequence block.
ZOOMScales the canvas output. 1:1 resets. In MISC.
RESRender scale: ½, ¾ or 1× of the display. In MISC.
RENDER CAPLimit on the render size: off, 1080 or 4K (default). Saved per device. In MISC.
resolution readoutRender size in use. ▼: the cap is reducing it.
param slidersThe slider area scrolls on its own.
Signal flow strip
GEN rowThe generative chain: eight boxes drawn left to right over black. Empty boxes are skipped. Click a block to show its params. CAM, CL and SEQ blocks sit over the boxes before them.
FX rowThe effects chain: eight boxes, applied left to right.
drag to reorderDrag a block to another box; an occupied box swaps.
drag from gridDrag a grid button onto a box: an empty box takes it, a filled one is replaced. A GEN can appear more than once, each with its own seed.
double-clickDouble-click a block to bypass it, again to restore.
top rowRND, SEQ, camera toggle, BANK, OVR, CLR. Rows below: clips, GEN, FX, presets.
preset rowOne block per saved preset; the loaded one is lit. Click to load. [ and ] step. Eight per page.
BANKNext page of presets (shown past eight).
NEWStarts a fresh patch: SAVE…, DISCARD or CANCEL. GEN and FX return to the default; clips, the STEPS row and clip settings stay. CAM, CL and SEQ blocks are removed.
HELPOpens this help (?).
OVROverwrite a preset: tap OVR, tap the preset, tap it again to write. Escape or OVR cancels.
CLRLit preset: deletes it. None lit: deletes all (tap twice). Escape cancels. No undo.
what a jump restoresLoading a preset restores everything it saved, including CAM, CL and SEQ blocks. Loaded clips stay; missing named clips are asked for.
drag off stripDrag a block off the strip to remove it. The last GEN shader stays.
empty boxClick an empty box, then a grid button, to fill it.
Source
CLIP rowHolds up to eight clips; not drawn. Drag a clip onto a GEN box for a clip layer, or add SEQ to play the STEPS row. Selecting a clip shows its settings under PARAMS: open, rotate, flip, mirror, speed, in/out, blend, amount.
no clipsWith no clip loaded the shaders run alone, with the camera if CAM is on.
ENTERShows the CAM, CL and SEQ blocks alone, or all blocks. Needs a clip loaded.
Clips
open fileOpen a video or image file.
drag & dropDrop a video or image onto the canvas to load it.
imagesImages load as 30-second clips; animated GIF, WebP, APNG and AVIF keep their animation (Safari: first frame). Max 3840 px; transparency becomes black. Needs WebCodecs.
in/out points0 key or in→out→clr: set in, set out, clear. Drag a handle to move a point, the playhead to scrub. Clip layers have their own in/out.
flip V / rot 90°Flip vertically or rotate 90°. Applies to the clips, or the camera with CAM selected.
GEN shaders
tap GEN buttonPuts the shader in the selected GEN box; blend and amount are kept, params reset.
drag to stripDrag a GEN button onto a box to place it.
multi-layer blendBLEND row at the top of a GEN block's params: drag for amount, pick the mode from the list.

clipA clip as a GEN block (CL1–CL8). Drag from the CLIP row or pick clip in the grid. Plays its own copy between its own in/out at the clip's speed. Params: BLEND, AMOUNT, IN / OUT. 0 sets in, out, then the whole clip. Bypassed or muted: pauses.
sequencePlays the STEPS row as a GEN block (SEQ). Each clip step once between its in/out; camera steps for their length; CLIP-row fade between clip steps. Own blend and amount. Bypassed: pauses.

Parameters
param sliderDrag sideways to change; tap to select. Toggles switch on tap. Choice params are lists (outlined yellow while modulated). Double-click: default. Angles show 0–360°.
pattern centringGrid effects (bitcrush, ascii, halftone, flow-dots) are centred on the frame.
clip slotsEight clip slots. Tap CLIP to open files; tap a clip to select it, an empty slot to load one. Drag onto a GEN box for a clip layer. Double-click: mute. MUTE: all. CAM: camera (shift+9). shift+1–8 select; ; ' step. The end blocks set the sequence fade length (CUT or 0.25–4 s) and fade blend. Clips are not saved in presets.
sequencerEight-step sequence of clip slots, played by a SEQ block. Drag clips onto steps; drag to swap or off to clear. Tap a step to jump. Each clip step plays once between its in/out. Camera steps run 2–60 s (tap to change). SPACE pauses. Saved when remember clip names & sequence is ticked.
snapping slidersSome params snap to fixed positions; the row shows the value. Two-position params are toggles. All can be modulated.
audio badgeCycles none, bass, mid, treble, volume. Scaled by AUD AMT and influence.
LFO badgeCycles L1, L2, L3, none. Scaled by influence. Adds to audio.
MIDI badgeBinds a MIDI control; see MIDI. MIDI sets the base; audio and LFO add.
influence0–100, drag up or down. Scales LFO, audio and MIDI on that param.
LFOs
3 LFOsThree LFOs, each with shape, AMT and period, moving bound params around their slider value.
shapessine, triangle, ramp, square, sample-and-hold, cycle +, cycle −. Cycles wrap continuously one way; PER is one pass, influence sets speed, AMT unused.
SKWSkew: wave timing. 0.5 symmetrical.
CRVCurve: wave shape. 0.5 linear.
previewShows the movement of a bound param.
AMTDepth, −1 to +1. Negative inverts. Clamped at 0 and 1.
OFSPhase offset, 0–1 cycle. Kept by SYNC.
SEC modePeriod in seconds, 0.1–30.
BPM modePeriod as a tempo division, 1/8–16 beats.
TAPTap to set the tempo (up to eight taps; resets after 3 s).
SYNCCopies LFO 1's period and mode to LFOs 2 and 3 and restarts all three.
yellow tickShows the modulated value; the bar shows the slider.
MIDI
device pickerPick a MIDI device. The dot is green when connected.
channelMIDI channel 1–16, or All.
MIDI badgeType a CC number (0–127) or N for note pitch and press Enter, or move a control.
note pitchN follows the last note: C2 (36) = 0, C6 (84) = 1. Holds after release.
bound badgeShows the CC (e.g. C7) or N. Tap to rebind; Escape cancels.
stackingMIDI sets the value; LFO and audio add; clamped 0–1; scaled by influence.
persistenceMIDI bindings are saved in presets and share links.
Audio modulation
off / mic / vid / fileAudio source. mic: microphone. vid: the selected clip's soundtrack (analysed, not played). file: an audio file, played aloud with its own controls.
audio file controlsPlay/pause, restart, loop, open, name and time. IN / OUT: drag markers to set the loop, to the end to clear; clr clears both. Loop points snap to zero crossings. Not saved in presets.
if a source will not startThe toast shows why. A blocked microphone is allowed from the address-bar padlock.
audio suspendedAmber suspended: tap or press a key to start audio.
vid, by browser and by transportvid is not available in Safari or on iOS. Clip layers and sequences are not heard.
nothing is movingThe status line shows the source and whether it is suspended.
BASS / MID / TRE / VOLBASS 20–250 Hz, MID 250 Hz–4 kHz, TRE 4–16 kHz, VOL loudness. Each normalised to its last ~4 s.
AUD AMTAudio depth, −1 to +1. Right pushes up, left down. The slider is the value in silence.
RELEASEFall time after a hit, 10 ms–4 s. All bands.
Blend modes
stack orderGEN boxes draw 1 to 8 over black. CAM, CL and SEQ blocks sit over earlier boxes.
blend rowGEN block mix amount. 0 skips it (except the first block).
mode listnormal, add, screen, multiply, difference, lighten, darken, overlay, dodge, burn.

normal vs the restGEN blends cost no extra pass. FX at normal and 1.00 cost none; anything else adds a pass.
